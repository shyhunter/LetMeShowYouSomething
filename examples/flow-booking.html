What I need you to decide

When someone cancels more than 24 hours before, should they get store credit instead of their money back?

Cancelling more than 24 hours before frees the slot for someone else, but the money stays with us as store credit — the customer is not charged again, and we are not out of pocket.

My recommendationKeep store credit rather than a refund, and say so on the confirmation screen before the person confirms, not after.

Where this has been done before

  • Class passes at gyms A cancelled class returns a credit to the pass, not money to the card; the rule is shown when you book. www.gov.uk
  • A studio a colleague worked with They refunded everything to the card and lost the slot as well; the rebooking rate never recovered. unverified · I could not find a source

What could go wrong

  • Customers may read "credit" as "refund" and ask for the money back anyway.
  • Credit that expires is the part people complain about; nothing here says whether it does.
  • Cancelling less than 24 hours before keeps neither the slot nor the money, which is the harshest step in the flow.
